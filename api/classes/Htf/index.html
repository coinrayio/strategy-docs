Class: Htf

Another timeframe and/or symbol (request.timeframe, request.security). Every member is a Series indexed in that context's bars: [0] is the DEVELOPING bar (it repaints until the bar closes), [1] is the last CLOSED bar. Use [1] for signals. Indicators accept these directly: ta.ema(ctx.close, 200) runs over the context's bars. Indicators without a source run on the context through its methods: ctx.atr(14), ctx.dmi(14, 14). ta.atr(14) itself always runs on the chart.
