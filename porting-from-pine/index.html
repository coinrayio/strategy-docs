Porting from Pine Script

The main differences​

  1. Declarations go at top level, calculations in onBar(). Pine runs the whole script per bar. Here the top level runs once and onBar() runs per bar.
  2. Types are explicit. f64 for prices, i32 for lengths. na is NaN.
  3. var needs care. A module variable behaves like Pine varip, not var. See the bar model before porting any var.
  4. History uses [n] on series only. To keep history of your own value, put it in a series().

Not supported​

table.*, matrix.*, request.security for another symbol, timeframes above 1 day, lower timeframes (request.security_lower_tf, or request.security to a timeframe below the chart's), lookahead_on, process_orders_on_close, libraries, and drawing getters such as line.get_price. Keep the values you need in your own variables instead of reading them back from drawings.
